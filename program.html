🗺️ Step 5: Investigations and Journeys

In this activity, you'll pair up and document your user journey through a website — just like real designers do!

💡 What is a User Journey?

A user journey is the path a person takes through a website. Designers document these journeys to understand user behavior and improve the design.

👤

The User

  • Navigate the site with a specific goal
  • Talk out loud about what you're doing
  • Describe what you see, click, and feel
📝

The Documenter

  • Listen carefully to your partner
  • Take notes on their journey
  • Use the log below to document
