🏗️ Step 1: The Web is a Designed Space

Everything you experience on the web is the result of a human decision and intention. A button, a search bar, text size, color choices — all are deliberately designed.

Think about it like buildings in the physical world:

🏚️

Haunted House

Dark, cramped, eerie — designed to scare

🏡

Cozy Cottage

Warm, inviting, comfortable — designed to relax

🤔 Think About It

  • ❓ What sites do you use most often?
  • ❓ What is the goal of each site? What does the creator intend for you to do?
  • ❓ Have you ever visited a site with one goal, but ended up doing something different?

🎯 Step 4: User Goals vs Site Goals

Websites have intentions — but so do you! Let's explore the tension between them.

🎯 YouTube's Goals

  • 📺 Encourage users to watch lots of content
  • 💸 Get users to click on ads
  • 💬 Encourage comments and engagement

👤 Your Goals as a User

  • 🎬 Watch a specific video
  • 📚 Find videos on a specific topic
  • 📤 Upload your own content

🧩 Match the Goal to its Owner

📈 Keep users on the site as long as possible
🔍 Find exactly what I'm looking for quickly
💲 Get users to make a purchase
🛒 Compare prices before buying

💡 Key Insight: When a site's design aligns with your goals, you have a great experience. When they conflict, frustration happens.
